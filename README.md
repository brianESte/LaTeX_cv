# Curriculum Vitae & Resumé

Some people treat the terms "resumé" and "curriculum vitae" as interchangeable, while others state that resumés are for non-academic positions, and CVs are for academic ones and include publications, and awards, among other things [1].
I prefer to treat a CV as a "full" resumé, a record of all schooling, work positions, accomplishments, with a resumé being a CV that has been filtered down to fit the specific position the owner is applying to.
